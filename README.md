# AuraPlan AI — Celebration & Event Planning Assistant ✨
**PromptWars Virtual [Challenge 5]: Celebration & Event Planner**

A GenAI-powered planning assistant that helps users organize **Weddings**, **Birthdays**, and **Community Events** with dynamic **Budgets**, **Master Timelines & Day-Of Run-of-Show**, **Vendor Checklists & Negotiation Scripts**, and **Personalized Ideas & Copywriting**.

---

## 🚀 Key GenAI Features

1. **Prompt-to-Plan GenAI Blueprint Studio**:
   - Switch seamlessly between **💍 Weddings**, **🎂 Birthdays**, and **🎪 Community Events** or use one-click inspiration presets.
   - Enter custom event parameters (Title, City/Venue, Date, Guest Count, Total Budget in `₹ INR` or `$ USD`, Aesthetic Vibe, and Natural Language Vision Prompt) to synthesize an end-to-end event blueprint.
2. **Smart Budget & AI Cost Optimizer**:
   - Multi-segment visual allocation bar, per-guest cost math, interactive sliders, and a one-click **"AI Optimize: Cut 12% Flexible Costs"** action.
3. **Chronological Timeline & Day-Of Run-of-Show**:
   - Filter between Pre-Event Countdown milestones and Hour-by-Hour Day-Of cues, toggle completion status, and add custom AI-tracked milestones.
4. **Vendor Checklist & AI Outreach/Negotiation Scripts**:
   - Track booking statuses (`Not Started`, `Shortlisted`, `Negotiating`, `Booked`), verify contract sub-tasks, review must-ask questions, and copy personalized **AI Price Negotiation Scripts** with one click.
5. **Personalized Ideas, Moodboard & AI Copywriter Studio**:
   - Generate new bespoke guest experience concepts, save ideas to your moodboard, and copy ready-to-send **Digital Invitations**, **Host Welcome Speeches**, **Event Hashtags**, and **Weather Backup Protocols**.
6. **Interactive GenAI Co-Pilot Drawer**:
   - Ask contextual questions about your active event plan (supports both **Google Gemini 2.0 Flash Live API** via the `API Key` button or `.env` `VITE_GEMINI_API_KEY`, plus a zero-config **Built-in GenAI Synthesis Engine** for instant reliability).
